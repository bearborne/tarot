# Card ideas — rough draft (learning notes)

Source studied: the Pinterest board "Amber Utopia" (Amber's Utopia keyword graphics), 154 pins. That covers all 78 cards, upright and reversed, **except The Devil** (it isn't on the board).

Everything here is paraphrased in our own words. Nothing is copied. Use it to learn the themes and pick what fits our philosophy.

- ✦ = gentle / growth-minded, candidate to keep
- ◐ = neutral, or depends on how it's framed
- ⚠ = negative / fear-based, likely skip or reframe (shortened to one line on most cards)
