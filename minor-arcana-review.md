# Minor Arcana: what to learn, what to cut

Source: the 56 Minor Arcana and court card pages on aeclectic.net (*minor-arcana/* and *court-cards/*), read 2026-11-20. Everything here is paraphrased. The rules and markings are the same as in [major-arcana-review.md](major-arcana-review.md): **✓ Learn** fits the rules (precise, positive, beneficial, psychological, symbolic); **✂ Cut** breaks one, and the rule is named.

## How the Minor pages differ from the Majors

- **There's no story section.** The Minor pages give keywords, upright, reversed, feelings and person, plus the card's scene. Only a few pages tie one card to the next:
  - **Cups 4 → 5:** the man who sulked over three cups loses them, and two still stand behind him.
  - **Wands 5 → 6:** the competition ends and the winner rides in to applause.
  - **Pentacles 3 → 8, the craftsman's arc:** he wins patrons (3), holds tight to what he earned (4), loses it (5), is helped (6), waits for a new chance (7), and starts again as an apprentice, this time for himself (8).
  - **Wands 10:** passion gets weighed down by everything that grows around it.
- **The court cards are almost all stereotypes about people,** such as the bully, the troll, the miser and the manipulative mother. Most of that is cut. What's left is each court card's *gift*.
- **There are more cuts than on the Majors.** The worst of them are suicide, addiction, abuse and predators, which are attached to the Cups courts, the Seven of Cups and the Nine of Cups.

## The patterns to cut

1. **Predictions:** trips by car, boat, plane or motorcycle, weddings, pregnancy, promotions, eviction, lottery wins and tax bills, inheritances.
2. **Medical and psychiatric claims:** suicidal thoughts (Seven of Cups R, Knight of Cups both ways), depression, bipolar disorder, postpartum depression (Queen of Cups R), chronic fatigue (Queen of Wands R), a stroke or memory loss (Queen of Swords R), learning disabilities (Knight of Swords R).
3. **Accusations about others:** cheating partners, bullies, trolls, hackers, thieves, embezzlers, the abusive father (King of Cups R), the jealous partner who treats you as property (Four of Pentacles).
4. **Doom:** "everything is lost", "you will NOT get your wish", "the pain never drains", "the worst only lingers", "eviction".
5. **Harmful advice:** "be the thief, use flattery or lies" (Seven of Swords), "a bullying coach is a good leader" (King of Wands).
6. **Trivia:** films, Crowley, Galileo, Martha Stewart, Virginia Woolf, soccer moms, the Godfather.
