# Hard themes, reframed

Every ⚠ line from [card-ideas-draft.md](card-ideas-draft.md), with a way to say it that fits the app's rule: **comfort and resolve, not doom.**

The reframe keeps what the card means. It doesn't pretend the hard thing isn't there. It just moves the focus from what goes wrong to what you can notice, choose or heal.

## The five moves

| Move | Instead of… | Say… |
|---|---|---|
| **Name the need** | "Burnout, exhaustion" | "Your energy is asking for rest." |
| **Turn blame into a boundary** | "People working against you, gossip" | "Protect your peace; not every voice needs an answer." |
| **Make it temporary** | "Loss, rock bottom" | "This is a season, not the whole story." |
| **Point to the choice** | "Indecision, stuck" | "You're allowed to pick one small next step." |
| **Turn it inward with kindness** | "Ego, control, manipulation" | "Where are you holding on too tightly — and why?" |

Lines marked **✂** are ones I'd drop rather than reframe. They're accusations about other people (fraud, abuse, cheating) that a reading can't fairly make.
