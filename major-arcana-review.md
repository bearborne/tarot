# Major Arcana: what to learn, what to cut

Source: the 22 Major Arcana pages on aeclectic.net (*tarot-card-meanings/major-arcana/*), read 2026-11-20. Everything here is paraphrased.

Each card's **story**, **upright** and **reversed** meanings are checked against our five rules:

| Rule | A line passes if it… | It fails if it… |
|---|---|---|
| **Precise** | names one clear idea | hedges, wanders into deck trivia, or lists unrelated things |
| **Positive** | leaves a way forward | ends in doom, no hope, or "nothing can be done" |
| **Beneficial** | helps the reader notice, choose or heal | scares, blames, or gives orders that could harm |
| **Psychological** | is about an inner state, need or choice | predicts outside events, diagnoses illness, or accuses other people |
| **Symbolic** | comes from the card's picture, number or correspondences | has no link to the card's imagery |

**✓ Learn** = worth bringing into `cards.js` (story, ideas, or a reframe). **✂ Cut** = fails a rule; the rule is named.

---

## The patterns to cut, across all 22 cards

Most of the cuts fall into eight groups. Most of what's left fits our rules well, especially the stories.

1. **Literal predictions** (psychological): car trips and car accidents, a baby boy or twins, money arriving, promotions, court papers, hospital stays, layoffs.
2. **Medical and psychiatric claims** (beneficial, psychological): medication, therapists, bipolar disorder, addiction and rehab, dementia, suicidal despair, agoraphobia, sunstroke.
3. **Death taken literally** (positive): "yes, it can mean a real death", an elderly relative "not long for this world".
4. **Accusations about other people** (psychological): con men, predators, stalkers, corrupt judges, a cheating lover, a nasty aunt, a demanding ex.
5. **Fate with no way out** (positive): "no hope", "the attempt will fail", "nothing can be done but accept it", "never a true whole".
6. **Advice that harms** (beneficial): "be aggressive", "manipulate", "it's good to be the puppetmaster", "run down anything in your way".
7. **Deck history and trivia** (precise): old card names, Crowley's titles, Justice and Strength swapping numbers, film references, a named criminal.
8. **Gendered or politicised framing** (precise, beneficial): "the male power of creation", pregnancy answers "for a woman", riots and national politics.

## 0 · The Fool

**Story** (no previous card → leads to The Magician)
- ✓ He carries everything he owns in one pack and is full of visions. A small dog at his heel warns him of the cliff. *(symbolic: the pack, the dog, the edge)*
- ✓ He hands his pack to the Magician, who lays it out on the table. That's a lovely bridge: **the potential the Fool carries becomes the tools the Magician uses**.

**Upright**
- ✓ A fresh start and infinite possibility. He already has everything he needs; he only has to stop and unpack.
- ✓ Feeling light, free, given a second chance. Not knowing where you're going is fine; enjoy the way.
- ✓ The dog as your instincts or a caring friend: listen to the voice that says "watch your step".
- ✂ "Often a literal move to a new home or job" (psychological: an outside prediction).
- ✂ "A business deal or new lover may be less sure than they look" (beneficial: suspicion about others). *Reframe:* stay curious and keep your eyes open.

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked: hard to start again or see with fresh eyes. Sitting by the road with stones in your shoes is a precise, kind image.
- ✓ Lost in your head: plans that haven't touched the ground yet.
- ✂ "Potential disaster", "falling off the cliff with no footing" (positive: doom).
- ✂ "Foolishness that only makes sense to himself" (beneficial: mocking).

## I · The Magician

**Story** (from The Fool → leads to The High Priestess)
- ✓ He unfolds the Fool's pack and reveals the four tools: Wand, Cup, Sword, Pentacle. Did he make them, or were they always in the pack? He won't say. **That mystery is the card's heart: your gifts were already yours.**
- ✓ Bridge: what to *do* with the idea is the next card's question.

**Upright**
- ✓ Creation through will and focus; making real what you imagine and say aloud.
- ✓ The four tools are the four Aces: raw, undeveloped power of each suit. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ The "reveal": the idea was in your head all along.
- ✓ A time of eloquence, wit, inventiveness.
- ✂ "The male power of creation" (precise: gendered).
- ✂ Thieves, snake-oil men; "check the magnetic person isn't a con man" (psychological: an accusation).
- ✂ A list of careers: scientist, salesman, politician… (precise: trivia).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked: the materials are there but not the plan; the words won't come. *Reframe:* choose one tool and use it today.
- ✂ The con artist, empty promises, a teacher who takes your money (psychological: accusations).
- ✂ "What was possible becomes impossible", "loses everything" (positive: doom).
- ✂ "The doctor struggles with a diagnosis" (beneficial: medical).

## II · The High Priestess

**Story** (from The Magician → leads to The Empress)
- ✓ The Magician's quiet opposite: seated, still, at night. The Fool lays out his tools and can't decide; she says nothing and hands him scrolls. An inner voice asks what his instincts say. *(symbolic and psychological, very strong)*
- ✓ She keeps the curtain closed: "we'll meet again when you're ready". This sets up The Moon later on.

**Upright**
- ✓ Not deciding yet: hold the decision while you listen to your inner voice.
- ✓ Quiet time alone; knowing yourself before acting.
- ✓ Pomegranate curtain → Persephone, journeys down and back. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ The Magician reveals; she keeps hidden. Moonlight to the Hermit's lantern. *(good links between cards)*
- ✓ An idea held safe while it grows: incubating, not yet born.
- ✂ Crystal balls, tea leaves, "talking with spirits" (precise: off our psychological frame).
- ✂ "A reclusive relative who knows family secrets", "may seem cold or scary" (psychological: about others).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked intuition: nothing comes through and the two choices look identical. *Reframe:* the voice is quiet, not gone.
- ✂ "A nasty aunt who uses family dirt", "a teacher who bites your head off" (psychological: accusations).
- ✂ "Loss of control, chaos, a kind of madness" (beneficial: mental-health scare).

## III · The Empress

**Story** (from The High Priestess → leads to The Emperor)
- ✓ The Fool hurries forward, impatient to see his creation fully grown. She tells him new things are fragile and need patience, love and attention.
- ✓ The sequence of cards is a gift: **Magician = the spark, High Priestess = choosing its form, Empress = where it grows, Emperor = managing it.**

**Upright**
- ✓ Nurturing anything into being: art, love, a project. Patient, generous care.
- ✓ Venus (beauty, gardens, artists: clay to statue) and Demeter (abundance). *(symbolic)*
- ✓ Shadow, said kindly: you can overwater as easily as neglect. Give growing things room to crawl, run and play. *(beneficial)*
- ✂ "A positive answer when a woman asks if she can get pregnant" (psychological, beneficial: a fertility prediction).
- ✂ "Possessive, jealous, smothering" as a label for a person (psychological). *Keep it only as the overwatering image.*

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked: you've lost the instinct for how to help something grow, and may need outside help. *Reframe:* turn your care toward yourself.
- ✂ "A parent ignoring a child running wild" (beneficial: blame).
- ✂ Uprooted forests, a ruined home or family, "abuse or violence" (positive: doom; psychological: accusation).

## IV · The Emperor

**Story** (from The Empress → leads to The Hierophant)
- ✓ The creation now needs managing. He rules from a stone throne through a clear will and solid foundations. The Fool leaves ready to lead rather than be led.

**Upright**
- ✓ Structure, boundaries, self-command: over your time, temper and habits.
- ✓ Mars to the Empress's Venus; order to her creativity. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ Aries as the enthusiastic, direct infant sign. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ A good check-in: has what you built become a chore? You may lead it differently, or step down.
- ✂ "Permission to be aggressive" (beneficial: harmful advice). *Reframe:* permission to be clear and bold.
- ✂ "A despot who imposes his will", "a feared tyrant" (positive: doom; psychological: about others).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked: authority that won't land, a household slipping into mess. *Reframe:* structure that holds you rather than grips you.
- ✂ "A Nero-like dictator" (psychological: accusation).
- ✂ "A manager is let go and the branch collapses" (psychological: a work prediction).
- ✂ "No one is in charge and no one can be" (positive: fatalism).

## V · The Hierophant

**Story** (from The Emperor → leads to The Lovers)
- ✓ Having built something, the Fool is suddenly afraid of losing it. The Hierophant offers two ways out: **release what you fear losing, or notice what you'd keep even if you lost it**. That's a precise, psychological gem.
- ✓ Shared traditions keep a community alive after people part. The Fool plans lessons and traditions with friends.

**Upright**
- ✓ Shared values and rituals that steady you in frightening times. *(Taurus: harmony, peace)*
- ✓ His contrast with the High Priestess: communal rites against solitary ones.
- ✓ Keep traditions to keep the spirit alive, not out of habit.
- ✓ "Remember where you came from and you'll know how to get through this." "You are never alone."
- ✂ The aside on organised religion stirring strong feelings (precise: off topic).
- ✂ "Unquestioned authority" as a good thing (beneficial).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Rules followed by rote, without understanding why. *Reframe:* trust what rings true from within.
- ✓ Being too harsh on others' differences (turned inward, kindly).
- ✂ "You may be in danger for being different or blowing the whistle" (positive: fear; psychological: an outside prediction).
- ✂ Institutions hoarding power, rich leaders and poor followers (precise: politics).

## VI · The Lovers

**Story** (from The Hierophant → leads to The Chariot)
- ✓ At a crossroads, the Fool sees someone by the fruit tree on the other path and feels a deep recognition, like meeting his missing half. He leaves his plan and chooses the new road. **The card is a choice of the heart against the plan.**

**Upright**
- ✓ A recognition more than a romance: being drawn to a person, path or work that feels meant for you.
- ✓ Following it may mean leaving your planned road, and common sense still has a say. *(precise, beneficial)*
- ✓ "Love" was the card's oldest name. *Keep as one line at most.*
- ✂ "You have met or will meet…" (psychological: prediction).
- ✂ "Even someone else's partner", "selfish, dangerous" (beneficial: invites harm and blame).
- ✂ "Refusing may bring lasting regret" (positive: fear).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked: you can't choose and can't hear your intuition. *Reframe:* what do you each really value?
- ✓ Choosing for the wrong reasons: worth asking why you want what you want.
- ✂ "A break-up that leaves both sides weaker" (positive: prediction).
- ✂ "Not soul mates", "will never form a true whole" (positive: fatalism; psychological: judging a relationship).

## VII · The Chariot

**Story** (from The Lovers → leads to Strength)
- ✓ He's nearly done what the Magician started, but his own confusion and obstacles stop him. The charioteer's advice: set one clear goal and **keep your dark and light horses pulling together**. Victory is a beginning, not an end.

**Upright**
- ✓ Opposing drives brought to pull one way; unity of purpose. *(symbolic: the two horses)*
- ✓ New motivation getting a stalled thing moving.
- ✓ Mixed feelings? Take the driver's seat and choose the direction.
- ✓ The warning, kept gently: winning isn't everything.
- ✂ "A trip, usually by car" (psychological: prediction).
- ✂ "Run down anything in your way", "hard-won victory over enemies" (beneficial: aggression).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked: you know the goal but not the way, and wonder what's the use. *Reframe:* direction matters more than speed.
- ✂ "Predicts a car accident", "car trouble" (positive, beneficial: a frightening prediction).
- ✂ "Cowardice", "total loss, stuck, can't lead anyone" (beneficial: shaming; positive: doom).

## VIII · Strength

**Story** (from The Chariot → leads to The Hermit)
- ✓ Proud from his victory and full of hot passions, the Fool sees a maiden gently close a lion's jaws; it obeys because she asked. **Wild energy can be banked like a hearth fire, guided and still satisfied.** *(This contrasts beautifully with the Chariot's control.)*

**Upright**
- ✓ Gentle mastery: understand, accept and soften the beast; don't chain it, flee it or kill it.
- ✓ Courage, perseverance, compassion. A steadfast friend.
- ✓ Like tending a sacred flame: respect the fire and it serves you.
- ✓ You'll get scratched a little along the way, and that's all right. *(honest, kind)*
- ✂ Deck trivia: "Power", "Lust", Justice and Strength swapping numbers, Daniel and Aesop (precise).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked: the strength is there, but courtesy or pressure keeps you from using it.
- ✓ Feeling timid about changing your life. *Reframe:* strength can be soft; rest rebuilds it.
- ✂ "Cowardice, no backbone" (beneficial: shaming).
- ✂ "An addict who needs rehab", "a cry for help", "the maiden needs saving" (beneficial: medical; positive: desperation).

## IX · The Hermit

**Story** (from Strength → leads to Wheel of Fortune)
- ✓ He withdraws to a little house in the woods. At dusk he walks with only staff and lantern, and **the lantern shows him night creatures and flowers he'd never noticed**. By lighting the world he lights his own mind, and becomes a Fool again, lit from within. *(It closes the loop to card 0; lovely.)*

**Upright**
- ✓ Going inward: a time to think, sort and take stock rather than act or decide.
- ✓ Virgo: introspection and careful analysis. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ Quiet lets the pieces fall into place; you come back understanding more.
- ✓ Long walks, a retreat. *(beneficial)*
- ✓ Some restlessness during withdrawal is normal.
- ✂ "Grumpy, antisocial, dismissive of others" (beneficial: a label).
- ✂ "A therapist may appear, unknown to friends and family" (psychological: prediction).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked: the lantern is shuttered, you feel alone in the dark, and **you can relight it**. *(positive, keep exactly this)*
- ✂ Misanthropic, paranoid; the named criminal (psychological; precise: trivia).
- ✂ "Mental decline, poor memory, an elder giving away money" (beneficial: medical).
- ✂ "Total darkness, unable to find your way" (positive: doom).

## X · Wheel of Fortune

**Story** (from The Hermit → leads to Justice)
- ✓ He comes out of hiding into sunlight as the wheel lifts him. Gifts arrive: a cup, a sword, coins. Good things he did come back to him.

**Upright**
- ✓ Some things turn beyond our control; we rise and fall with the wheel. *Reframe:* hold plans lightly.
- ✓ Jupiter: luck, change, what goes around comes around. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ A welcome turn, a long-awaited break.
- ✂ "Money, a job, a promotion, a marriage proposal", "you've won a lottery" (psychological: prediction).
- ✂ "Repaid threefold" (precise: an overclaim).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked: the wheel wants to turn but is stuck, and stillness can feel harder than a downturn. *(precise, psychological)*
- ✓ If something outside freezes change, you can choose to step away. *(beneficial)*
- ✂ "Bad luck; someone else gets your promotion; you may be laid off" (psychological: prediction; positive).
- ✂ "Nothing to do but accept it" (positive: fatalism).
- ✂ "The Sphinx falls, Set rises, luck is unreliable" (positive: ominous).

## XI · Justice

**Story** (from Wheel of Fortune → leads to The Hanged Man)
- ✓ A blind, wise woman splits an inheritance between rich and poor brothers. Neither is happy; both are better off. The Fool sees he has fed his worldly goals and starved his spirit, and **sets out to rebalance**.
- ✓ Justice opens the second half of the journey: from the outer world to the inner one.

**Upright**
- ✓ Balance by reason: what you need, not always what you want.
- ✓ Adjustment, not punishment or moral judgement. *(keep this exact point)*
- ✓ Libra; the sword as a clear, sometimes uncomfortable cut. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ Overdoing anything (work, exercise, indulgence) costs you elsewhere. Rebalance body, feelings, social life and spirit.
- ✂ "A court case or legal papers" (psychological: prediction).
- ✂ "Drinking or drugs harm health" stated as a warning (beneficial: medical). *Fold into "overdoing costs you".*

**Reversed**
- ✓ Getting too much or too little of what you need: imbalance. *Reframe:* look gently at what's out of balance, including in yourself.
- ✂ "Biased judge, corrupt lawyers, tampered evidence" (psychological: accusations).
- ✂ "One selfish person blocks fairness" (psychological: blame).

## XII · The Hanged Man

**Story** (from Justice → leads to Death)
- ✓ He fasts, keeps still, and hangs upside down. For a moment he lets go of everything, and **the coins falling from his pockets look like plain metal**. His view of the world has changed; now he must right himself and act on it. *(symbolic, precise)*

**Upright**
- ✓ A new perspective: problems solve themselves when you see them upside down.
- ✓ Suspension; a waiting room that's also a meditation.
- ✓ Number 12 against The World's 21: infinitely in, infinitely out. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ Odin's nine days for the runes; a baby turning before birth. *(symbolic: keep one image)*
- ✓ Small versions count: dropping a prejudice, trying something new.
- ✓ Once you've seen it this way, you never see it quite the same again.
- ✂ "The Traitor" (precise: trivia; and it's a negative label).
- ✂ "Sacrifice of money, time or self" (beneficial: martyrdom). *Reframe:* surrender the struggle, not yourself.

**Reversed**
- ✓ Clinging to the old view; not ready to let go.
- ✓ Blocked: insights you can't yet bring back into everyday life.
- ✂ "Selfishness, ego" (beneficial: blame).
- ✂ "You gain nothing because you won't endure the pain" (beneficial, positive).

## XIII · Death

**Story** (from The Hanged Man → leads to Temperance)
- ✓ The Fool crosses a bare winter field feeling he's lost something. Death rides up at sunrise and says yes, his old self is gone. **Mourning is natural; old leaves fall before new ones grow.** *(Best of the stories. Keep the sunrise.)*

**Upright**
- ✓ An ending and how you meet it: relief and heartbreak both take time.
- ✓ Scorpio: scorpion, serpent and eagle as stages of transformation. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ Something new comes to fill the gap; winter turns to spring; a phoenix.
- ✓ Grieve first; the sorrow isn't permanent.
- ✂ "Yes, it can mean a real death", "an elderly relative isn't long for this world" (positive, beneficial, psychological: all three).
- ✂ "A pet fish dies, a restaurant closes" (precise: trivial).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Something that should end drags on; you can't grieve, so you can't begin again. *Reframe:* let go at your own pace.
- ✂ "A hospital patient kept alive" (beneficial: medical, distressing).

## XIV · Temperance

**Story** (from Death → leads to The Devil)
- ✓ The Fool, healing, looks back at the opposites he's met: Justice's scales, being hung between worlds, death and birth. An angel, one foot in water and one on land, pours between two cups and **the two blend into something new**. Only lack of will keeps opposites apart, and he's been keeping his own worlds separate.

**Upright**
- ✓ Moderation, not giving things up: water in the wine.
- ✓ Tempering: iron becomes steel through heat and cooling. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ Fire (red) and water (blue) make violet; opposites can merge. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ You don't have to give up either side; mix until it's right, bit by bit.
- ✓ Takes patience and experiment.
- ✓ Sagittarius: the centaur, bow and arrow working together. *(symbolic)*
- ✂ "Crowley called it Alchemy" (precise: trivia).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked: the parts won't mix yet, like oil and water. Not enough of one ingredient; unblock it and the method appears. *(positive, keep)*
- ✂ "The formula goes horribly wrong; peace turns to war" (positive: doom).
- ✂ "You're told the attempt will fail; even the drive is fading" (positive: fatalism).

## XV · The Devil

**Story** (from Temperance → leads to The Tower)
- ✓ On a black mountain the Fool feels his own desires rise. The goat-god says it only shows what's already in him, which is nothing to fear or be ashamed of. **The collars are loose: the people stay by choice.** Others used the same drive to climb to the peak. *(Excellent: precise, psychological, symbolic.)*

**Upright**
- ✓ Chains that are only in the mind; feeling powerless when you aren't.
- ✓ Be honest about what holds you and what empowers you.
- ✓ Pan or Dionysus, not Satan. *(symbolic, reassuring)*
- ✓ Capricorn: the mountain goat climbing to the top; ambition. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ The power to change lies with you, not with the temptation.
- ✓ The strong one chooses when to indulge and when to walk away.
- ✂ "Sometimes it's good to manipulate", "become the puppetmaster" (beneficial: harmful).
- ✂ "Chocolate, sex or heroin", "the addict or stalker" (beneficial: drugs; psychological: accusations).
- ✂ "The most dangerous card" (positive: fear).

**Reversed**
- ✓ A near escape: a pull resisted, a breather. The chains fall off. *(positive)*
- ✓ Too much restraint: afraid to take a chance, or to love.
- ✂ "In danger from someone dangerous" (positive, psychological: an accusation about others).

## XVI · The Tower

**Story** (from The Devil → leads to The Star)
- ✓ He meets a tower he once helped build to prove he was better than others, and sees the old self still in him. He shouts; lightning; the tower falls. Fear, and also **a strange clarity: the lies are gone and the foundation of truth remains**. He looks back over the journey and knows he can rebuild.

**Upright**
- ✓ A belief that turns out false; a flash of truth.
- ✓ Clearing what wasn't built to hold you so you can build on what's true, or choose not to rebuild. *(positive, beneficial)*
- ✓ A need for change ignored too long. *(psychological)*
- ✓ Mars: lies against truth. *(symbolic)*
- ✂ "Your lover isn't faithful, your faith is wrong, your family isn't fine" (psychological: accusations; positive).
- ✂ "Falls suddenly and violently, all at once" (beneficial: frightening).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Avoiding a truth you half know; relief that may not last. *Reframe:* the change is inward this time.
- ✓ Wanting the truth out but not able to say it yet.
- ✂ "The liars win, you're seen as the liar, you suffer while the tower stands" (positive: doom; psychological: accusations).
- ✂ "Exposing a scam or scandal" (precise: off topic).

## XVII · The Star

**Story** (from The Tower → leads to The Moon)
- ✓ He sits on the cold ground, his life revealed as partly a lie. A woman with two jars refills the pool and waters the earth; he eats, drinks, and his heart heals. **She fades and her light becomes a star: follow it.** *(keep, beautiful)*

**Upright**
- ✓ Hope and healing after a hard time; help from unexpected places (a friend, a book, music).
- ✓ Aquarius; a slow, gentle card, with no sudden change. *(symbolic, precise)*
- ✓ What you want most is possible; keep it in sight, as it takes time.
- ✓ "What do you want enough to go the distance for?" *(a great question; use it)*
- ✂ "Someone isn't interested now but will be", "you won't succeed today" (psychological: predictions).
- ✂ "No guarantees, you may be lured away" (positive: undercuts the card).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked: hope is there but behind cloud. *(keep: "the stars are still there")*
- ✂ "No future, no hope, false hope leads you astray" (positive: doom).
- ✂ "A sick person won't recover", "dying or suicidal" (beneficial: medical; dangerous to show).

## XVIII · The Moon

**Story** (from The Star → leads to The Sun)
- ✓ Night, a watery path, feeling half asleep. He passes between the pillars, **behind the High Priestess's veil at last**. Wolves, strange creatures, and a boat with no oar: be lost, be hunted, or trust the river. He trusts it, and visions come. *(Great link back to card II.)*

**Upright**
- ✓ Dreams, intuition, creativity; flashes that are oddly accurate.
- ✓ Not a time for decisions that need clear, rational thought.
- ✓ An emotional, moody stretch: pour it into art or writing.
- ✓ Pisces. *(symbolic)*
- ✂ "Hidden enemies" (psychological: accusation).
- ✂ "Mental illness, blackouts, bad drug trips", "take your medication, see your therapist" (beneficial: medical advice; must never appear).
- ✂ "Genius and psychopathy", "wild drunks" (beneficial: romanticises illness).
- ✂ "Untrustworthy whirlwind romances" (psychological: prediction).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked: creative block; a calm that is held, not settled. *Reframe:* the fog is lifting.
- ✂ "Criminals, sexual predators, stalkers" (psychological; must never appear).
- ✂ "Depression, bipolar disorder, breakdown, addiction" (beneficial: medical).
- ✂ "Riots, revolutions, anarchy" (precise: off topic; positive).

## XIX · The Sun

**Story** (from The Moon → leads to Judgement)
- ✓ After the night he wakes in a walled garden. A laughing child on a white pony shows him seeds, insects and light on the water, and asks simple, deep questions. **The child is his own new self; the garden and the sun are inside him.** *(keep)*

**Upright**
- ✓ Your day in the sun: joy, clarity, simple pleasures.
- ✓ The light of the waking mind; muddles clear and things fall into place.
- ✓ Warmth that others feel from you; a good time to decide.
- ✓ The gentle warning: too much sun burns, so don't ignore the shadows. *(beneficial)*
- ✓ "Everything is going to be all right." *(keep as the card's spirit)*
- ✂ "A baby on the way, likely a boy or twins" (psychological: prediction; the page itself says it rarely happens).
- ✂ "Money arrives, maths errors corrected, you get the credit" (psychological: predictions).
- ✂ "Liars come clean" (psychological: about others).
- ✂ "Fainting from sunstroke" (precise: odd).

**Reversed**
- ✓ An eclipse: things look dim and muddled, but the light comes back. *(positive, keep)*
- ✓ Forgetting to play; losing the child's view. *Reframe:* you don't need to perform brightness.
- ✂ "Problems with pregnancy or young children" (beneficial: medical).
- ✂ "Ignorance and cynical adulthood win", "everything wilts" (positive: doom).

## XX · Judgement

**Story** (from The Sun → leads to The World)
- ✓ One last step, but he can't take it until he makes peace with his past. He blows the trumpet; his past selves rise, even the ones he tried to forget. **He forgives them, they forgive him, and they drift off as memories with no hold on him.** *(Excellent; the core of the card.)*

**Upright**
- ✓ Old things resurface to be faced, forgiven and laid to rest.
- ✓ Forgive or be forgiven; do the long-postponed thing.
- ✓ Fire; rebirth. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ Not Death: a change you choose, not one forced on you. *(precise)*
- ✓ Courage: what was buried is out in the open now.
- ✂ "A hospital stay", "healing from an accident" (psychological: prediction; beneficial).
- ✂ "Leaving an abusive spouse" (psychological: assumes abuse).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Not able to let go yet; something you thought was done comes back. *Reframe:* answer the inner critic with kindness.
- ✂ "Graves spitting out the dead" (positive: ominous).
- ✂ "No escape, no hope of a new future" (positive: doom).
- ✂ "A recurring illness, a demanding ex, debts blocking housing" (psychological: predictions and accusations).

## XXI · The World

**Story** (from Judgement → back to The Fool)
- ✓ His last step brings him back to the very cliff he nearly walked off as the Fool, and now he sees it differently: body and mind, self and world are one. **He steps off smiling and dances in a ring of stars.** The journey ends where it began. *(Keep: it closes the circle with card 0.)*

**Upright**
- ✓ Completion, wholeness, satisfaction.
- ✓ Saturn: competence, student to teacher. *(symbolic)*
- ✓ Everything comes together; feeling at home anywhere.
- ✂ "A PhD, a book tour, a trip around the world, starting a business" (psychological: predictions).

**Reversed**
- ✓ Blocked: something stops you finishing. *Reframe:* one last step closes the circle.
- ✓ Staying in your comfort zone.

## What's worth bringing into the app

1. **The Fool's story, card by card.** Every card has a short scene and a clear bridge to the next. That fits a "story" line on each card page (from ← · → to).
2. **Links worth showing:** Fool ↔ World (the same cliff), High Priestess → Moon (behind the veil), Chariot ↔ Strength (control against gentleness), Hermit → Fool (lit from within), Death → Temperance (healing after loss).
3. **Questions to borrow:** the Star's "what would you go the distance for?" and the Hierophant's "what would you still have if you lost it?"
